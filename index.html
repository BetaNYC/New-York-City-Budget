NYC Council discretionary funding: the Schedule C explorer is frozen

The explorer that was here is frozen and no longer updated. We are rebuilding the underlying data and code. Its figures were extracted from the text of the Council's adopted-budget PDFs, and BetaNYC no longer stands behind that extraction. Please do not cite figures from it.

You followed a link into the frozen explorer. That view is still online, frozen and marked do not cite, at legacy.html.

Where the data comes from now

BetaNYC rebuilt its NYC Council budget data on structured sources only:

  • Discretionary expense designations: the Council's own expense funding disclosure spreadsheets, FY2009 and FY2011 through FY2027, one row per published designation, with clearance status and the grantee's mailing address. FY2010 is not included: the Council's archived workbook for that year is incomplete against NYC Open Data's. FY2009 and FY2013 are early snapshots, frozen while many designations were still pending.
  • Capital designations: the Council's own capital workbooks and NYC Open Data, FY2015 through FY2017 and FY2019 through FY2027. FY2018 is missing from every source found.

Every row says which file it came from and when that file was downloaded.

Use it

  • Ask an AI assistant: the @betanyc/nyc-budget-mcp server, version 2.0. Its README covers setup and what each tool answers.
  • Download the data: the New-York-City-Budget repository on GitHub. The disclosure is in data/fyNN/expense_disclosure/ with per-year sources in data/disclosure_years.csv; capital is in data/capital/.
  • What changed and why: the 2.0.0 changelog.
