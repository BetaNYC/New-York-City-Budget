Our first Council explorer has moved

This page was our first Council discretionary funding explorer, from July 2026. It showed Schedule C by category and by agency, with figures an older parser read from the Council's budget PDFs. We found errors in that parser's output, froze the page on October 16, 2026, and have now retired it. Please do not cite figures you copied from it.

Council discretionary funding is now in the budget explorer, read again from the Council's own spreadsheets and PDFs and checked against every printed total.

Open Council discretionary funding

Old links still work: a link to a year, a category, an agency or an initiative opens the explorer's Schedule C search for the same year and name. If a name is not found there, the search shows the whole year.
