NYC budget data, checked

BetaNYC publishes New York City's budget as data you can search and check. There are two halves, and they answer different questions. Every number comes from the City's own data, and we never change one by hand.

City agency budgets

How much each City agency was given in the adopted budget, and how it changed during the year, FY2017–FY2027. Every agency and community board, by unit of appropriation.

Answers: How much does my community board get? How did an agency's budget change?

Source: NYC Open Data, Expense Budget (mwzb-yiwb), published for the Mayor's Office of Management and Budget (OMB). FY2026 adopted: $115.9 billion net; $117,791,473,676 as counted in Open Data, which includes $1,884,163,949 of intra-city money.

Checked: the data build stops unless it matches OMB's printed FY2026 Adopted Budget to the dollar.

Council discretionary funding

Which organizations the City Council funds, for how much, through which agency, and at whose request. Expense awards FY2009 and FY2011–FY2027, capital designations, and the tables from the Council's budget PDFs.

Answers: Which groups did my Council member fund? Did this nonprofit get Council money?

Source: the Council's spreadsheets and budget PDFs, and NYC Open Data.

How the Council's PDF tables are checked

We read every row of the Council's FY2018–FY2027 budget PDFs twice, once from the PDF's text and once from a picture of the page, and tied the rows to every total the PDFs print. Where the two readings or the totals disagree, a person reviews it. We never change a number: values are shown exactly as printed.

  • 141,456 rows in 36 tables: Schedule C, the transparency resolutions, the capital book and Terms and Conditions.
  • Schedule C: 76,216 of 76,272 rows read the same both ways. The other 56 went to a person.
  • 515 of 519 printed totals tie exactly. The other 4 are totals the PDF itself gets wrong, and they are flagged, not fixed.
  • The review is still going. Every row is labeled "checked, review in progress" until a person has decided every flagged item for its year. No year is finished yet.

Figures as of the pdf-tables-2026-10-24-260d16e data release. FY2017 and earlier are not re-read yet.

The Council's spreadsheets and NYC Open Data

  • Discretionary expense designations: the Council's own expense funding disclosure spreadsheets, FY2009 and FY2011 through FY2027, one row per published designation, with clearance status and the grantee's mailing address. FY2010 is not included: the Council's archived workbook for that year is incomplete against NYC Open Data's. FY2009 and FY2013 are early snapshots, frozen while many designations were still pending.
  • Capital designations: the Council's own capital workbooks and NYC Open Data, FY2015 through FY2017 and FY2019 through FY2027. FY2018 is missing from every source found.

Use it

  • Look up agency budgets: the City agency budget explorer, every agency and unit of appropriation, adopted and modified, FY2017–FY2027, with the data as one CSV.
  • Search Council funding in your browser: the NYC Council budget explorer, built from the same data as the MCP server below. Every row links to the file or PDF page it came from.
  • Ask an AI assistant: the @betanyc/nyc-budget-mcp server. Its README covers setup and what each tool answers.
  • Download the data: the New-York-City-Budget repository and its data releases.
  • What changed and why: the changelog.
