NYC budget data, checked

About

We publish New York City's budget as data you can search and check. There are two halves, and they answer different questions: what each City agency is given, and which organizations the Council funds. Every number comes from the City's own data, and we never change one by hand. How it works, every source, and the glossary.

City agency budgets

How much each City agency was given in the adopted budget, and how it changed during the year, FY2017–FY2027. From NYC Open Data, checked against OMB's printed FY2026 Adopted Budget to the dollar.

Look up agency budgets

Council discretionary funding

Which organizations the Council funds, for how much, through which agency, and at whose request. FY2009 and FY2011–FY2027, from the Council's spreadsheets, its budget PDFs, and NYC Open Data.

Search Council funding

Spending and the official budget

Was the money spent? The Comptroller's Checkbook NYC tracks City spending and contracts. The official budget documents come from the Mayor's Office of Management and Budget (OMB).
