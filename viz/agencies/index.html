City agency budgets have moved

Every City agency's adopted and modified budget, by unit of appropriation, FY2017 on, is now in the budget explorer. If this page does not send you there, follow this link:

Open City agency budgets

Old links still work: a link to an agency, a unit or a year opens the same agency, unit and year there.

What is different

  • A unit that is not in a year's budget now says "Not in this year's budget". This page used to show $0 there. The numbers are the same: 1,737 amounts it showed as $0 (936 adopted and 801 modified) were units that were not in that year's budget.
  • Agencies are grouped by agency number, so the Department of Consumer and Worker Protection (agency 866) shows once, under the name in each year's budget, not twice under its old and new names.
  • The same figures are in the budget MCP server (search_agency_budget, compare_adopted_modified).

Still here

  • Download every agency, unit and year (CSV). Same address and columns as before.
  • Community boards: every board's budget in one table.
