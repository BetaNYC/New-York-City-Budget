Where each number comes from

Adopted is the budget adopted in June, before the fiscal year starts. Modified is that year's budget as changed during the year, as of June, near the year's end. The fiscal year runs July 1 to June 30 and is named for the year it ends.

Adopted FYY is financial_plan_amount in the June release tagged Y; Modified FYY is current_modified_budget_amount in the June release tagged Y+1. FY2027's Modified figure is not yet published: the City publishes it when it adopts the FY2028 budget, normally in June 2027. Each row of the CSV names the release each figure comes from.

Personal Services is pay for the board's staff. Other Than Personal Services is everything else. Most boards also have a unit for Rent (or Rent and Energy). The total adds a board's units.

These are budgets, not spending. For what a board actually spent, search for it on Checkbook NYC, the Comptroller's site. OMB's printed budget is on OMB's publications page; Manhattan Community Board 1's FY2025 and FY2026 figures here match page 182E of OMB's FY2026 Adopted Budget, Expense, Revenue and Contract volume.

Before 2026-11-09 the agency explorer showed every year one year late (the correction). This table uses the corrected years.
